Why do seemingly obscure answers only grant 15 points? Understanding the psychology behind the game's greatest player frustration.

⚠️ Anatomy of a "Too Clever" Trap

In Krillion, when thousands of players try to "outsmart" the prompt by picking what feels like a non-obvious pick, they all end up congregating on the exact same entry. The game detects this statistical spike and downgrades it to the Too Clever tier (15 points).

❌ The "Too Clever" Trap (15 Pts)
  • Prompt: California City
  • Player Thought: "Everyone will say LA or SF! I'll be smart and say Sacramento or Berkeley!"
  • Result: 42% of players had that same thought. COLLISION!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Krillion (The Daily Dive)?

Krillion is a web-based reverse-trivia daily game. Every day, all players receive the same 7 prompts and have 25 seconds per prompt to type in a valid answer. Points correspond to depth in meters: common answers yield 4–10 points, while rare, esoteric answers grant up to 100 points, descending your submarine up to 7,000 meters into the ocean trench floor.

What are the six Krillion rarity tiers?
  • Plankton (4–10 pts): The obvious majority pick (e.g., LA for California city).
  • Too Clever (8–15 pts): Overthought answers where many trivia fans collide.
  • Schooler (16–30 pts): Mid-tier recognized choices.
  • Rare (32–60 pts): Less conventional valid choices.
  • Deep Cut (64–85 pts): Substantial niche knowledge.
  • One in a Krillion (100 pts): The rarest valid answer in the player pool.
How does typo tolerance work in Krillion Unlimited?

One major player complaint in the official game is that in high-pressure 25-second countdowns, typing a 9-letter obscure biological or geographical name often leads to an accidental typo and 0 points. In our Krillion Unlimited simulator, an intelligent Levenshtein fuzzy matcher detects single-letter slips and offers instant correction without unfair penalties.
