In the Krillion game, when thousands of players intentionally overthink a prompt to avoid the obvious choice (e.g., choosing Sacramento instead of Los Angeles for California), they frequently congregate on the exact same secondary candidate. Krillion Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Krillion game and how do you play?

The Krillion game is a web-based reverse-trivia daily challenge (also known as the daily dive). In every Krillion game, players receive 7 open-ended prompts and have 25 seconds per prompt to enter a valid answer. Points correspond to depth in meters: common answers yield 4–10 points, while rare, esoteric answers grant up to 100 points, descending your submarine up to 7,000 meters into the ocean trench floor.

How does typo tolerance work in Krillion Unlimited?

In high-pressure 25-second countdowns, typing a 9-letter biological or geographical name often leads to an accidental slip. In our simulator, an intelligent Levenshtein fuzzy matcher detects single-letter slips and offers instant correction without unfair zero-point penalties.

How does LocalStorage streak tracking work in the Krillion game?

Your daily streaks, personal maximum depth, and total dives are stored directly in your browser's private local storage. No account creation, passwords, or tracking cookies are required.

What is the difference between daily Krillion and Krillion Unlimited?

The daily Krillion game presents a single curated set of 7 prompts synchronized globally for all players each day. Krillion Unlimited is a free infinite practice simulator that lets you replay unlimited rounds with diverse categories at your own pace.
